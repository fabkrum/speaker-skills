# The method's reference texts

The rules, checklists and evidence behind *Better with every talk*, written once and used by
both the guide and the skills. The guide teaches the method in prose; the skills apply it to the
speaker's own talk. Where they overlap, these texts are the source.

Each text speaks about **the speaker**: whoever is preparing and giving the talk.

| Text | What it covers | Used when |
|---|---|---|
| [`talk-brief.md`](talk-brief.md) | Goal, one desired outcome, one next step, throughline, audience, format, room | Before the storyboard |
| [`story.md`](story.md) | Throughline, audience as hero, curse of knowledge, structures with contrast, stories and numbers, first and last minute; the early and final story checks | On the storyboard, and after the build |
| [`slides.md`](slides.md) | Image slides: a full image with one sentence on a blurred panel; words only for numbers, code and comparisons; speaker notes as cues; the build check | While building the deck |
| [`concision.md`](concision.md) | Why saying less wins, answer first, what over-explaining looks like, fixes | On slides, notes and recordings |
| [`accessibility.md`](accessibility.md) | Contrast, colour, motion, the file itself, and labelling AI-generated images | While building, and before sharing |
| [`delivery.md`](delivery.md) | Sizing up the audience, reading the room, voice, eyes, the first minute, when things go wrong, Q&A | Rehearsal and the day itself |
| [`style-file.md`](style-file.md) | The speaker's style file: look, scheme, colours, fonts, image kind, motif; its format and keys | Before the first deck, and on every build |
| [`rehearsal-rubric.md`](rehearsal-rubric.md) | Three recorded run-throughs, what to measure, the nine-dimension rubric, the report, the take log and the talk log, the manual checklist | After each recorded rehearsal |

## Sources and credits

The evidence-based sources keep their citations in each text: Chris Anderson's TED guide, Nancy
Duarte, Barbara Minto, Chip & Dan Heath, Randy Olson, Richard Mayer, Michael Alley, Garr
Reynolds, John Sweller, WCAG 2.2, the EU AI Act, and the research papers listed under
*Sources* at the end of each text.

Some techniques are inspired by two speaking trainers and rewritten here in this project's own
words, without their acronyms, templates or quotes:

- Inspired by Andy Harrington's speaker training: teaching one point deeply through the
  questions the audience is asking, a question on the slide as a cue for the speaker, a mental
  image per point for recall, eye contact per thought, and calming the body before going on.
- Inspired by TJ Walker's public speaking courses: researching the audience by asking
  attendees and the organiser in advance, and rehearsing in three recorded run-throughs with a
  purpose each.

Neither trainer has reviewed or endorsed this project.
