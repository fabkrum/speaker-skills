# Image prompts: Rehearse with your phone

Written from the style file (look, image kind, motif) for whatever image tool the speaker has.
Every prompt ends with the same frame so the pictures match. Until the images exist, the deck
uses placeholders.

Frame for every prompt: quiet, early-morning light, one object in focus, lots of empty space on
the side where the sentence sits, a small paper boat somewhere in the picture, wide 16:9.

1. Slide 1, photograph: a phone on a desk showing a paused video of a speaker mid-sentence,
   a small paper boat beside it; empty space on the left. Not AI if the speaker takes it.
2. Slide 2, AI photograph (label it): a speaker in their twenties wincing at their own
   recording on a laptop, seen from the side, a paper boat on the shelf behind; empty space
   bottom right.
3. Slide 3, photograph: a phone propped on a stack of books at eye height, an empty meeting
   room behind it; empty space at the bottom.
